---
title: A new front door for DOCLIX
date: 2026-07-26
summary: DOCLIX now has a real landing page, a live search demo, and a friendlier way to explain what this thing actually does.
---

For a while, going to DOCLIX with no account meant hitting a sign-in wall. That's fixed now — there's a proper landing page that explains what DOCLIX is before asking anyone to sign up for it.

The centerpiece is a live search bar. It's not a mockup — it queries the same public index that powers ⌘K everywhere else in the app. Type a real term from any public project and you'll get real results, right there on the homepage.

We also gave buttons around the marketing pages a bit more life: floating pill shapes, a soft lift on hover, and a primary call-to-action that gently drifts instead of sitting completely still.

More is coming. This post is post #1 for a reason.
